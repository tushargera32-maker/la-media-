# ✅ PRESS CAROUSEL - CENTER FOCUSED WITH BLUR

## 🎯 NEW DESIGN:

### **Center-Focused Carousel:**
- ✅ **Active card:** Sharp, full size, center stage
- ✅ **Adjacent cards:** Scaled down (90%), blurred (2px), faded (40%)
- ✅ **Far cards:** More scaled (85%), more blur (4px), more fade (20%)
- ✅ **Auto-rotate:** Every 4 seconds
- ✅ **Navigation dots:** Click to jump to any card
- ✅ **Smooth transitions:** 700ms duration

---

## 🎨 EFFECTS:

### **Center Card (Active):**
- Opacity: 100%
- Scale: 1.0 (full size)
- Blur: 0px (sharp)
- Z-index: 10 (on top)

### **Adjacent Cards (+/- 1):**
- Opacity: 40%
- Scale: 0.9 (90% size)
- Blur: 2px
- Z-index: 5

### **Far Cards (+/- 2+):**
- Opacity: 20%
- Scale: 0.85 (85% size)
- Blur: 4px
- Z-index: 1

## 🎪 USER INTERACTION:
