## ABOUT BUILD RIGHT ADVISORS

### Overview
Build Right Advisors is a comprehensive construction advisory service operating under the LA Media & Communications group. We provide expert guidance covering every stage of your building project, from initial planning to post-construction support.

### Mission
To empower clients with expert knowledge and professional guidance, ensuring their construction projects are executed right—from foundation to finish.

### Core Philosophy
We believe in comprehensive, stage-by-stage advisory that addresses not just construction challenges, but also spatial design, cultural considerations (Vastu), and long-term project success.

### B. Common Questions Addressed

**Q: How is Build Right different from an architect?**
A: We complement architects by providing specialized advisory, Vastu expertise, and ongoing construction guidance. We work alongside your architect, not instead of them.

**Q: Do you provide construction services?**
A: No, we provide advisory only. We guide you in selecting and managing contractors but don't execute construction ourselves.

**Q: Can you help with ongoing projects?**
A: Absolutely. Our Construction Issue Resolution service is designed specifically for projects already underway.

**Q: What areas do you serve?**
A: Primarily Ludhiana and Punjab, with select projects across North India.
